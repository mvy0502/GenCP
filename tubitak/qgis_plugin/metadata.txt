description=Generate a georeferenced synthetic 10 m reference raster from OSM and CLC+ over a chosen extent.
about=Renders OpenStreetMap vectors over a CLC+ Backbone land-cover base in the GenCP visual convention, runs a pix2pix generator via onnxruntime, and mosaics the result into a single georeferenced GeoTIFF on a 10 m grid. Adds the result as a QGIS layer and/or writes it to disk. Matching and GCP extraction are out of scope - a separate application consumes this output. All generation logic lives in gencp_core, which imports neither Qt nor QGIS.
